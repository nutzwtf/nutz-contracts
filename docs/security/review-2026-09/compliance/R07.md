## R07 — ETH→USDG Leg floored on-chain by the Distributor's `minUsdgPerEth`

> "Mitigation: keeper on a dedicated host, KMS-held key, and the ETH→USDG Leg (98% of every Sweep) floored on-chain by the Distributor's Signer-set `minUsdgPerEth` (§2.2 step 4); the stock Legs are bounded by one `perStock` each."
> — docs/spec/engineering-spec.md §6, "Threat: keeper key compromise"

> "4. ETH→USDG in one swap of `ethIn − opsAmt` through `routes[1]`. Not isolated: a Venue failure re-raises the Venue's own revert data, and `usdgOut < (ethIn − opsAmt) × DISTRIBUTOR.minUsdgPerEth() / 1e18` reverts `UsdgBelowFloor`. The Distributor's Signer-set rate range (§3.3) is thus the on-chain floor under a compromised Keeper's `minOut` for the 98% of every Sweep that reaches Holders."
> — docs/spec/engineering-spec.md §2.2 step 4

**Verdict:** implemented · confidence: high

**What this demands of an implementation:** after the ETH→USDG swap, the Converter must compute `floor = (ethIn − opsAmt) × minUsdgPerEth / 1e18` from (a) the Distributor's *current* storage value, read at call time rather than cached, and (b) the ETH actually handed to the Venue, and revert the whole Sweep when the USDG received is below it. `minUsdgPerEth` must be settable only by the Signers (2-of-3), never by the Keeper.

**Where enforcement lives:**

```solidity
// src/NutzConverter.sol:L333
uint256 usdgOut = _ethToUsdg(routes[1], ethIn - opsAmt);

// src/NutzConverter.sol:L448-L452
function _ethToUsdg(Route calldata route, uint256 ethIn) private returns (uint256 usdgOut) {
    usdgOut = _runLegStrict(Leg.EthToUsdg, route, ethIn);
    uint256 floor = ethIn * DISTRIBUTOR.minUsdgPerEth() / 1e18;
    if (usdgOut < floor) revert UsdgBelowFloor(usdgOut, floor);
}
```
- `ethIn` inside `_ethToUsdg` is the caller's `ethIn - opsAmt` (L333), the exact amount the Venue receives: v3 sends `{value: amountIn}` (L613), v4 settles `{value: order.amountIn}` (L688) with `order.amountIn = amountIn` (L643). So the floor is over the Leg's actual ETH input.
- `DISTRIBUTOR.minUsdgPerEth()` is an external view on the immutable `DISTRIBUTOR` (L123), evaluated on every Sweep; the Converter stores no copy (the only Converter storage is L140-L146).
- `usdgOut` is the Venue's reported output: v3 the router's return (L613-L615); v4 the callback's `amountOut` (L646, L674-L676, L696), which is the manager's positive delta and is also `take`n to the Converter at L695.
- The rate's provenance: `NutzDistributor.sol:L98` storage, set in the constructor at L179-L184 (`minRate_ == 0` reverts `InvalidRateRange`) and by `setRateRange` at L423-L429 under `_require2of3` (Signers L121-L129). No Keeper path writes it; `setKeeper` (Signers L58) does not touch it.
- Units: the Distributor documents the rate as "raw USDG (6 decimals) received per 1 ETH; fee = wei × rate / 1e18" (L96-L97), and the Converter divides wei × rate by 1e18 (L450), the same convention.

**Paths walked:**
- `sweep` → `_ethToUsdg`: `ethIn − opsAmt` is never zero, so the Venue is always hit and the floor always compared. `ethIn == 0` reverts `NothingToSweep` (L331); `opsAmt = ethIn × 200 / 10000` (L436) is strictly less than `ethIn` for every `ethIn ≥ 1`, and is further clamped down by `headroom` (L437). ✓
- `_runLegStrict` (L575-L584): a Venue failure re-raises the Venue's own revert (L579-L581), so the Sweep cannot proceed to a floor check with `usdgOut == 0` from a caught failure. ✓
- v4 path: a zero-input early return exists in `_runV4` (L638-L641) but is unreachable here (input is non-zero, above). ✓
- The floor is compared after the swap, so a below-floor swap reverts the transaction and the ETH never leaves (the whole `sweep` reverts, including the Ops transfer at L439 and any Fee pull). ✓
- `convertAcorn` (L522-L544): no ETH→USDG Leg, so nothing to floor; not in this requirement's scope. —
- What the floor does not cover, consistent with the document: the Ops ETH (out at L439 before the swap, at most 2% and `opsCap ≤ 5 ether`), the NUTZ→ETH Leg (L420, `minOut` only), and the four stock Legs (`minOut` only, one `perStock` each).

**Searched:**
- `minUsdgPerEth` in `src/` → 5 hits: Converter L450 (the floor); Distributor L98 declaration, L184 constructor write, L389 `pushClaims` range check, L426 `setRateRange` write. No other writer.
- `UsdgBelowFloor` → L158 declaration, L451 revert. One site.
- `setRateRange` callers in `src/` → only the external entry at Distributor L423 (2-of-3).
- `Route.minOut` on the ETH→USDG Leg → v3 `amountOutMinimum: route.minOut` (L609), v4 `order.minOut` (L643) checked at L676. The Keeper's `minOut` is a second, independent bound; nothing in the contract requires `minOut ≥ floor`.

**How the verdict was reached:** the requirement is one comparison and it is present, over the right quantities, reading the right storage live. It is not `stronger-than-spec`: the document already states the exact formula. Confidence is high because every operand was traced to its source rather than to a name.

**Open questions:**
- None about the code. A document nit: §2.3 calls the range "the Distributor's Signer-set minimum rate as a second floor" while the range's primary purpose in §3.3 is the `pushClaims` fee rate; the same `minUsdgPerEth` serves both, which is what the code does.
